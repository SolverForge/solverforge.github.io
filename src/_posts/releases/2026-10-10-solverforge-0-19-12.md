## What Changed

### Occupied equality buckets stay in place

Equality index updates mutate occupied buckets in place rather than taking them
out of the map and inserting them again. Only empty buckets relinquish their
vectors to the retired buffer pool introduced in 0.19.11. Old-key retraction and
duplicate suppression remain intact. A counted-hash regression checks that
retracting from a surviving bucket probes once.

### Existence link bookkeeping stays in place

Semi/anti join updates append to retained left/right relationship lists in place,
using generation-checked handle storage rather than removing and reinserting each
list. Existing generation checks, old-state retraction, residual predicates, and
duplicate suppression remain required. Oracle-backed semi/anti join churn tests
cover key changes, generation reuse, and residual-predicate transitions.

This patch builds on 0.19.11's reusable relational buffers and exact indexed
equality extraction change; the Clarke-Wright interruption correction belongs to
0.19.11, not this patch's source delta. There are no new public modeling APIs or
scaffold targets. No measured performance gain is claimed, and historical site
benchmark evidence and tested versions remain unchanged.
