**solverforge 0.20.0 and 0.20.1** are published on crates.io and released on
GitHub (2026-10-12), following `solverforge 0.19.14`. `cargo add solverforge`
installs 0.20.1 and docs.rs serves the 0.20.1 API. The Rust `1.95` floor and
the constraint-stream surface are unchanged: both releases change how the
solver spends its work, not what models may declare.

## What Changed

### Grouped-scalar default search forages by accepted count (0.20.0)

The grouped-scalar default local search stepped with
`FirstLastStepScoreImproving` and no cap: each step stopped at the first
step-improving accepted candidate and discarded the remaining accepted work.
Grouped models now forage with `AcceptedCount(256)`, the same policy as list
models: a step sweeps until 256 candidates are accepted (or the union
drains) and applies the best of the sweep. Throughput no longer depends on
how quickly the first improvement appears. On the upstream n060w8 10-second
probe this ran 233 steps over 5,767 evaluated candidates against 73 steps
and 1,840 evaluations on the 0.19.13 defaults (3.1x steps, 3.1x depth per
budget); ta18 makespan and the X-n167-k10 parity tuple were unchanged. This
changes default search depth, not a configuration contract: applications
that pin their own forager keep their pinned behavior.

### Precedence scoring and cycle checks stop allocating per candidate (0.20.1)

Hypothetical-move cycle checks on the precedence route graph allocated seven
buffers per candidate, and the scoring constraint allocated a fresh
`RouteChange` per event. `PrecedenceRouteGraph` now owns reusable scratch
(staged post-move route, edge diffs, indegree, ready set, DFS buffers), its
cycle checks run over the graph's live successor lists, and
`ListPrecedenceState` pools its `RouteChange` through take/restore per
event. The scoring state re-times routes once per move — a retraction defers
its refresh to the matching insert, whose BFS seeds walk pending removals
and additions once. Cycle probes use a monotonic stamp instead of an O(N)
queue reset, and the makespan keeps a max-finish occurrence count so no full
rescan follows each incremental BFS. Scores, match results, and the public
list-precedence constraint surface are unchanged.

### Grouped-scalar construction pools its feasibility scratch (0.20.1)

`blockers()` allocated a fresh blocker list at every feasibility probe, and
`assignment_feasible_after_edits` reallocated the change log, touched
capacity keys, edited-pair list, and checked-pair list per attempt. The
state now owns pooled vectors taken and restored at each probe's nesting
depth, and `assignment_allowed` carries the probe scratch through as a
mutable borrow. Construction decisions are unchanged.

## Upgrade Checklist

- Update direct Rust dependencies and confirm `Cargo.lock` resolves `0.20.1`.
- Compare grouped-scalar solve quality and step depth at your time limit; the
  default search now sweeps up to 256 accepted candidates per step.
- Applications with explicitly configured foragers or phases need no change:
  the foraging change applies to the default grouped-scalar search only.
- The pooled-scratch fixes change no scores or decisions; re-running your
  regression suite is sufficient.
- Upgrade CLI-generated apps deliberately: CLI 3.3.3 still scaffolds core
  0.19.7; these releases do not retarget scaffolds, rebuild Python wheels, or
  update apps.
